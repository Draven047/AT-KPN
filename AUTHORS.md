# AT-KPN authors and credits

AT-KPN is a derivative font project developed for Amar in 2026. Its proportion changes, asymmetric stroke-end treatment, six independent Latin letter constructions, script-specific optical refinements, alternate Malayalam construction and additional Latin mark attachment were developed for the approved AT-KPN Study 02 direction.

The underlying Baloo 2 fonts are by **Ek Type**, copyright 2019. Their original author and contributor lists are retained in `sources/upstream/AUTHORS.txt` and `sources/upstream/CONTRIBUTORS.txt`.

Cocon by Evert Bloemsma was the user's visual reference. Cocon is not included, and its authors do not endorse this derivative of Baloo.
