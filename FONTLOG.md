# AT-KPN font log

## 0.700 — 24 November 2026

Fitted the Bold letterforms to the approved Study 02 concept image (`design/approved-concept.png`), so the eight greetings now read as the concept sheet. `tools/fit_concept.py` sets each greeting with the real font over the artwork and fits only clean, structure-preserving changes. The results are stored in `design/concept-fit.json`.

- **Letters:** each letter in the concept gets a smooth separable proportion warp. Stems stay vertical, curves stay smooth, and the baseline and headline are pinned.
- **Marks and subscripts:** the Hindi े and the Kannada and Telugu subscript ka get a free 2D reshape and repositioning. The Malayalam chandrakkala gets its own 2D shape and position. Dots (pulli, nukta, aspirate drops, the dot of র) are resized and moved without distortion and stay round.
- **Weight:** Bold weight is set per script from measured stroke thickness: +2 units for Malayalam and Bengali, +4 for Tamil, +10 for Telugu, unchanged elsewhere. Stroke weight now matches the concept within 0–6% in every script.
- **Corners:** every outer corner is softened to a fitted radius (18 units for English, 26 for the Indian scripts, scaled for Regular and Light) and inner corners to half of that. Glyphs whose shape would change are left untouched.
- **Devanagari headline:** the Bold headline is 8 units thicker on its underside.

These changes also reach glyphs that reuse a concept letter's contours (accented letters, conjunct parts), and the letter, mark and corner changes apply to Light and Regular so all three weights stay consistent.

**English Light and Regular (26 November 2026):** the `e` and the feet of `l` and `i` were drawn for the Bold stem, so in Light the left side and lower curve of `e`, its exit, and the `l`/`i` feet ran 30–60% thicker than the stems. They now scale with the stem (`tools/english_refine.py`), and their accented forms follow. In Light, the thickest point of `e` dropped from 1.50 to 1.20 stems (90th percentile 1.29 → 1.00, the same as `o`), and the `l` and `i` feet from about 1.55 to 1.04 stems. Bold is unchanged. `fit_concept.py --apply` now accepts `--scripts` and `--weights`, so a single regenerated component can be refitted without reapplying the fit to the others.

**English proportions (27 November 2026):** the English letters now follow the scalar reference proportions in `design/reference-profile.json` in every weight. Only measurements are used; no reference outlines are copied, and the letters remain AT-KPN's own drawings.

- Capitals are no longer raised by 710/690. Cap height is 685 units in Light and Regular and 690 in Bold (was 710).
- Each letter gets its own vertical factor below the baseline, so the descenders of g, j, p, q and y reach the reference depth (about 170 to 182 units, was about 210 to 225) and stem ends no longer dip 8 to 9 units under the baseline. Accented letters with marks below the baseline keep their mark height.
- The custom letters H, I, e, i, l and o take their box and advance from the reference for the weight, instead of using the Bold box in every weight. In Regular, `o` is 62 units narrower and `e` 42, and `H` matches the reference width.
- The concept letter warps are no longer applied to English (`NO_LETTER_FIT` in `tools/fit_concept.py`); English still gets the corner finishing. All 62 reference letters and digits in Regular and Bold now match the reference box and advance within 5 units (43 to 44 differed before).

Because English no longer follows the concept warps, its overlap with the Study 02 concept image is 0.89 (was 0.96); the mean over the eight words is 0.918. The Indian scripts are unchanged.

**Curves and edges (27 November 2026):** the blanket corner softening of this version is replaced by analytic finishing (`tools/outline_finish.py`). The softening re-traced every glyph through stroke and boolean operations, which blunted every corner alike and left outlines with scattered points, thousands of segments under 3 units and small kinks.

- Outlines keep their drawn nodes. Per glyph, the mean number of segments fell by about a third (English Bold 55 to 35, Kannada 101 to 76, Telugu 112 to 84). Segments under 3 units fell from 139,547 to 216 across the 21 sources, and kinked smooth nodes from 7,062 to 8.
- Stroke ends are again one crisp corner and one soft corner, in every script. Square stem ends (English stems and arms, Devanagari, Bengali and Tamil stems, bars) share one shape: a flat cut, a crisp corner, and a soft corner reaching 0.56 stem widths along the cut and 0.65 along the side, the shape already used for H, I, i and l. Feet stand on the baseline. Headline ends keep their drawn shape so headlines still join.
- Bold weight offsets and the Devanagari headline are applied as exact node offsets. Near-smooth nodes are made exactly smooth, extrema are put on the axes, and points inside straight lines are removed.

Overlap with the concept is unchanged within 0.002 (mean 0.917). `specimen/AT-KPN-edges-before-after.png` compares the two states. The complete download is 21.3 MB (was 23.5 MB).

**English M, W and w (27 November 2026):** these three letters are newly drawn in `tools/english_refine.py`, in every weight, from AT-KPN's own construction: one thick zig-zag stroke through the letter's turning points, with a width per stroke, cut level at the baseline and the top line. The centre V of the M now stands on the baseline (it stopped at about 40% of the cap height), and the middle apex of W and w now reaches the cap height and x-height (it stopped at about two thirds). Stroke feet and tops get the family's stem end (one crisp, one soft corner), the clipped joins get round corners, and the inner joins stay sharp. The letters fill the reference box and advance exactly, accents over W and w are rebuilt on the new forms, and no other glyph changed. `specimen/AT-KPN-MWw-before-after.png` compares old and new in each weight (old above new).

**English K, N, f, r and the ascender letters (27 November 2026):** K and N are newly drawn in `tools/english_refine.py` from straight stems and slanted strokes of a set width, cut level at the baseline and top line, with the family's stem-end corners. K's stem now has the same weight as the other capitals (in Bold it was about 14% heavier), its arm and leg are lighter (0.9 of the stem), each stops at the other's inner edge so the two meet in a point inside the stem, and the arm ends in a level cut at the cap height instead of a round knob. N's diagonal leaves the top of the left stem and lands at the foot of the right stem, a little wider in Bold. The stems of f and r are widened to the weight's stem (they were about 0.88 of it) by a smooth horizontal warp that keeps each letter's box. In b, d, h and k the bowl or arch now reaches the x-height in every weight: these letters had been scaled to their ascender height, which left the bowls of Regular and Light 18 to 30 units low; their x-height zone now takes the same scale as n and only the ascender is stretched. Ķ and Ņ take a comma below on the new K and N. The changed glyphs are K, N, b, d, f, h, k, r and their accented forms; no other glyph changed. P, e and s are unchanged. `specimen/AT-KPN-KPNfresh-before-after.png` compares old and new in each weight.

**English corners (28 November 2026):** the English letters and figures take the corner sizes of the reference sheet, in every weight. A corner is where two straight sides meet (stem ends, bar ends, feet, the inside angle of an arm), or where a straight side meets a curve (the shoulder of n, the bowl of u and b). For each corner the sheet gives three numbers: where the sides would meet and how far back along each side the rounding starts (0 for a crisp corner). They are kept in `design/reference-corners.json`, scalars only, no outline data. `tools/corner_match.py` matches each of those corners to the same corner of AT-KPN's own outline and redraws it: the sides are trimmed to the measured lengths and joined by one curve with the family's handle tension (0.65, the sheet's median). Where the sheet has a flat top or bottom between two corners (n, m, h, u), the arch gets that flat. A change is kept only if it stays a clean, local corner change. Letters without straight sides (o, O, 0, 8) and everything between the corners stay as AT-KPN draws them. About 240 of the sheet's ~390 corners per weight have a counterpart; their setbacks now differ from the sheet by 1 to 3 units on average (before: 24 to 29). Accented letters take their base letter's new corners. `specimen/AT-KPN-corners-before-after.png` compares old and new.

**English stroke ends and arches (28 November 2026, later):** the corner pass now reaches more letters. Arches and bowls with up to six curve pieces between the stem and the top or bottom (n, m, h, u, U, b, d, p, q, a, g, j) take the reference corner sizes. Rounded stroke ends on curves (c, C, s, S, e, a, f, g, r, t, j, J, 2, 3, 5, 7, 9) are now cut straight where the reference cuts them: the cut sits at AT-KPN's own stroke tip, flows out of the outer curve and meets the inner side in a crisp corner, so no letter changes size. The reference's cut lines are kept in `design/reference-corners.json` as two end points each. Steep diagonal strokes (A, V, v, y) take level cuts only where that adds a small wedge inside the letter's width; X, x and Y keep their square ends, because a level cut would widen their arms. The lowercase measurements are now taken from the baseline of n (x dips below the baseline).

**English K foot (28 November 2026):** K's leg no longer ends in a level cut with a round corner. As in the reference, its outer edge bends out into a sharp point a quarter stem beyond the leg, a little above the baseline (0.06 of the cap height in Light to 0.14 in Bold), one round curve sweeps from that point down to the baseline, and the inner edge curves into that bottom. Ķ follows. `specimen/AT-KPN-K-before-after.png` compares old and new.

Overlap with the concept (Bold, intersection over union, best size within 5%) rose from a mean of 0.86 in 0.600 to 0.93: English 0.96, Kannada 0.92, Hindi 0.95, Marathi 0.94, Malayalam 0.91, Tamil 0.92, Telugu 0.90, Bengali 0.92. The weakest letters from the detailed audit improved most: Kannada subscript ಕ 0.72 → 0.87, Hindi े 0.85 → 0.93, Hindi/Marathi न 0.91 → 0.96. `specimen/AT-KPN-concept-sheet.png` shows the concept sheet beside the same sheet set in the font, and `specimen/AT-KPN-concept-overlay.png` overlays each word.

OTF files now use whole-unit CFF coordinates (at most 0.5 units from the TrueType outlines) and are subroutinized, which keeps the complete download within hosting limits. A trial rebuild on Anek by Ek Type was evaluated and rejected because it moved further from the concept (mean overlap 0.80). The glyph set, shaping, the Malayalam ss01 option and character coverage are unchanged from 0.600.

## 0.600 — 23 November 2026

Implemented the approved visual audit across Light, Regular and Bold. English l/i feet now return into rounded terminals; e has a softer rising exit and asymmetric counter; cap-height balance and sample spacing are coordinated. Rebuilt Devanagari and Bengali headline ends and flattened internal headline dips. Rounded the Kannada upper-hook template in every glyph that shares it and balanced 86 below-base forms per weight. Telugu upper strokes now start with a flat ledge; the hanging Ka is broader and shorter. Tamil has circular dots and flat stem starts with rounded returns.

Malayalam now draws the Sa–Ka construction inline using the font's Sa, virama and Ka outlines. Standard input remains unchanged. The previous stacked form remains available through ss01, with its own spacing and retained anchors. The virama uses a rounded U shape. The default substitution glyph name remains stable, preserving the native shaping sequence.

Each weight contains 3,615 glyphs and 1,291 Unicode mappings. Revised editable UFOs, retained layout TTX, build tools, WOFF2, TTF and before/after proofs are included. Source provenance and the SIL OFL remain unchanged.

## 0.500 — 23 November 2026

First full family built from the approved Study 02 direction: Light, Regular and Bold; unified English, Kannada, Hindi, Marathi, Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu and Bengali. Baloo 2 supplies the open-source native foundations and shaping. Native widths, advances and anchors are coordinated at 87–93% of their source widths. Small alternating convex corners receive crisp cuts while broad bowls remain soft. Six Latin letters have independent outlines, their accented forms are coordinated, and Latin combining marks receive explicit placement.

The package includes TTF, WOFF2, 21 editable UFOs, 21 retained layout TTX files, selected original VFB/layout sources, build tools, actual-font proofs, interactive specimen and QA reports. Prior Noto-based AT-KPN versions remain archived separately.
