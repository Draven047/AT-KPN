# AT-KPN font log

## 0.600 — 23 November 2026

Implemented the approved visual audit across Light, Regular and Bold. English l/i feet now return into rounded terminals; e has a softer rising exit and asymmetric counter; cap-height balance and sample spacing are coordinated. Rebuilt Devanagari and Bengali headline ends and flattened internal headline dips. Rounded the Kannada upper-hook template in every glyph that shares it and balanced 86 below-base forms per weight. Telugu upper strokes now start with a flat ledge; the hanging Ka is broader and shorter. Tamil has circular dots and flat stem starts with rounded returns.

Malayalam now draws the Sa–Ka construction inline using the font's Sa, virama and Ka outlines. Standard input remains unchanged. The previous stacked form remains available through ss01, with its own spacing and retained anchors. The virama uses a rounded U shape. The default substitution glyph name remains stable, preserving the native shaping sequence.

Each weight contains 3,615 glyphs and 1,291 Unicode mappings. Revised editable UFOs, retained layout TTX, build tools, WOFF2, TTF and before/after proofs are included. Source provenance and the SIL OFL remain unchanged.

## 0.500 — 23 November 2026

First full family built from the approved Study 02 direction: Light, Regular and Bold; unified English, Kannada, Hindi, Marathi, Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu and Bengali. Baloo 2 supplies the open-source native foundations and shaping. Native widths, advances and anchors are coordinated at 87–93% of their source widths. Small alternating convex corners receive crisp cuts while broad bowls remain soft. Six Latin letters have independent outlines, their accented forms are coordinated, and Latin combining marks receive explicit placement.

The package includes TTF, WOFF2, 21 editable UFOs, 21 retained layout TTX files, selected original VFB/layout sources, build tools, actual-font proofs, interactive specimen and QA reports. Prior Noto-based AT-KPN versions remain archived separately.
